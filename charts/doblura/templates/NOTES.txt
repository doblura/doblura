Before your first rehearsal you need two things Doblura does NOT create:

  1. An anonymized dump of production, on a PVC, in S3, or anywhere else.
     Producing it is your problem and your regulator's: Doblura consumes it.

  2. A Postgres server whose user has CREATEDB. Every rehearsal creates its own
     scratch database and drops it when it finishes.

Check that it started:

    kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "doblura.fullname" . }}

And launch the first one:

    kubectl apply -f https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aromera/doblura/v{{ .Chart.AppVersion }}/config/samples/odoorehearsal-volume.yaml
    kubectl get rehearsal -w

Remember: neutralize defaults to true, and it should stay that way. A production
dump restored without neutralization sends real invoices to real customers.
